Claim Your Board

Prove you're standing at the board by power-cycling it a couple of times. This takes a few minutes — no rush.

Step 1 — Find the label on the board

Look for the sticker on the underside of the board. Type the code exactly as printed.

Step 2 of 2 — Power-cycle the board now

Unplug the board, wait a few seconds, then plug it back in. Leave this page open — we'll pick it up automatically.

round 1 of 2

You have about 12 minutes left. It's fine to close this and come back — the code stays on the label.

We couldn't confirm you were at the board

This is the same message whether the attempt is still waiting, didn't go through in time, or expired — it never says why, so it never gives anything away about the board itself.

note: FR76.9 — the board is always named by what's printed on it, never "device id"; the code field is meant to be pasted, not memorized. FR76.3 — round progress ("round 1 of 2") is shown to the claimant they marked themselves, but no round's individual pass/fail is ever disclosed. The terminal card's copy is identical for waiting-too-long, a failed round, and an outright expiry (FR76.9), and offers "invite me" (FR75) and "support reference" (FR80) as prominently as retry — never a lesser, buried option.
